About

PhD Applicant · Johns Hopkins SAIS & National University of Singapore

I am a PhD applicant holding dual Master's degrees: an M.A. in International Economics and Finance from the Johns Hopkins University School of Advanced International Studies (SAIS) in Washington, D.C. and a Master in International Affairs from the Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy (LKYSPP), National University of Singapore. I previously earned a B.Econ. and B.A. from the China Foreign Affairs University in Beijing.

My research interests center on labor market inequality, technological change and AI development, and political economy, with a particular focus on how macroeconomic policy — especially monetary and fiscal policy — shapes economic outcomes. I work across quantitative methods, applying instrumental-variable estimation, time-series modeling, and unsupervised machine learning to questions in political participation, comparative growth, and public-sector labor markets.
